# Spec — a hub client owns its connection for its whole life

**Contract.** A `HubClient` retains the Vox connection that drives its consumer lane, including across the lazy redial already promised by `shim-redials-a-dropped-hub`. Dropping the client releases the connection; replacing a dead lane releases the old connection. Repeated calls on one client do not dial, and repeated clients do not leave established sockets behind after they drop.

**Chosen.** Connection ownership belongs to `HubClient`, beside lane ownership and redial. Process composition roots may share one client, but ownership and cleanup must also be correct when they do not.

**Rejected.** Fixing only the console and waker call sites while leaving a dropped `HubClient` unable to close its transport. A process-global client as the only ownership mechanism.

**Escape hatch.** Short-lived commands may still construct their own client; its drop closes the transport.
