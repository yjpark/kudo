# Spec — a blocked submit waits on named items instead of finishing its item

**Contract.** `submit` may carry `blocked_on`, a non-empty list of items in the claim's context. It records the worker's log and reason, settles the claim as `blocked`, and leaves the context head unchanged. The claimed item becomes ready again only after every named item has submitted; it is never finished by the blocked settlement. The named items are validated like enqueue dependencies and become waiting conditions for the claimed item. A normal submit and a check verdict remain incompatible with `blocked_on`.

**Chosen.** A typed blocked outcome on submit, because the worker has already attempted the item and its reason belongs in the submission log. Waiting conditions live in the settlement because they were discovered during that attempt.

**Rejected.** Treating the word "blocked" in a log as completion. Immediately releasing the item to spin without a readiness condition. Arbitrary executable or natural-language conditions in the scheduler.

**Escape hatch.** `release` remains the unconditional give-back for work that should be ready immediately; a later normal submit finishes the blocked item.
