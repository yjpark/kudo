# Spec — an item is ready only after every item it depends on submits

**Contract.** An item may name one or more earlier items in the same context with `enqueue --after <item>`. It is not ready, and no worker can claim it, until every dependency has a `submitted` settlement. Due time and dependencies both gate readiness. A missing item, another context's item, a duplicate, or a dependency on itself is refused at enqueue; because dependencies are write-once and must already exist, cycles cannot be introduced.

**Chosen.** Explicit item ids in item metadata, interpreted by the same readiness rule used by pull, the Stop hook, ready counts, and the waker. A dependency means submitted, not merged or checked: those are different completion conditions and may become separate dependency kinds later.

**Rejected.** Textual guards in the ask. Worker-side enforcement after a claim. Treating release or a blocked settlement as satisfying a dependency.

**Escape hatch.** An item with no dependencies retains the existing behavior.
